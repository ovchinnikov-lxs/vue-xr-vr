## After-every-task rule: wrap-up skill, hooks, CI check

### Done
- `wrap-up` skill, Stop hook that blocks ending a session with work not in the
  journal, SessionStart hook that shows the last "Next" and pending commits,
  CI `traceability` job that reports commits without an issue reference or a
  journal entry.

### How
- The Stop hook compares commits after the last commit touching `journal/`
  and uncommitted changes; it lets the stop through on the second attempt
  (`stop_hook_active`) so it never loops.
- CI reports as warnings and a job summary instead of failing, so a missing
  journal line never blocks deploys or releases.
